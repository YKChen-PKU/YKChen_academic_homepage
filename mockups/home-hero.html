§ IIIRESEARCH THEMES
Spectrum from quark models

Tetraquark bound states and resonances using constituent quark models, complex scaling, diffusion Monte Carlo — including fully heavy $QQ\bar{Q}\bar{Q}$ states.

Hadron-hadron interaction

Coupled-channel dynamics for $X(3872)$ pole structure; virtual states; three-body molecular states like $DD^*\bar{K}^*$.

Electromagnetic properties

HBχPT calculations of polarizabilities for spin-$\tfrac{1}{2}$ and spin-$\tfrac{3}{2}$ singly heavy baryons and heavy triplet hadrons.

Production of exotic states

Branching fractions in $B^- \to D^- X_{0,1}(2900)$ and hidden-charm strange molecular states in $B$, $B_s$ decays.
